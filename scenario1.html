Our Support Team
How Support Teams Work Today

Four Systems. Zero Cross-Visibility. Always Reactive.

Without the platform, Divya manages the HUL account the same way every other support team does — one system at a time, always one step behind the problem.

System 1 of 4
Logs into o9 for demand signals
Separate login. Exports demand variance data manually. No way to know if the forecast deviation is creating a stockout — that data lives in Relex, a different system entirely.
System 2 of 4
Logs into Anaplan for supply plan status
Checks PO pipeline and replenishment plans. A delayed PO shows up here — but she can't see whether the delay is caused by a carrier issue. That's in the TMS.
System 3 of 4
Logs into Relex for warehouse health
Inventory levels, days of cover, stockout risk. Relex shows the warehouse is at risk — but it doesn't know why demand is spiking. That signal is in o9.
System 4 of 4
Requests a TMS report from HUL's logistics team
Gets a weekly carrier performance file over email. By the time she reads it, the damage is done. No one has connected the carrier delay to the stockout risk in Relex.
The Real Problem
Nobody connects the dots. HUL calls us when it's already too late.
Every system shows its own fragment of the picture. o9 sees demand going up. Anaplan sees a delayed PO. Relex sees stock running low. TMS sees Blue Dart failing on a critical lane. But no single system — and no human being manually logging into four portals — can see all four simultaneously and understand what they mean together.
"HUL's Supply Chain Director calls us on a Thursday afternoon. A stockout has already hit. We spend two hours across four systems trying to explain what happened. We're telling them the story of a fire that already burned."
