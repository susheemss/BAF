System 2 of 4
Logs into Anaplan for supply plan status
Checks PO pipeline and replenishment plans. A delayed PO shows up here — but she can't see if a carrier issue is causing it. That's in the TMS.
System 3 of 4

Inventory levels and days of cover. Blue Yonder flags the risk — but doesn't know why demand is spiking. That signal is in Kinaxis.
System 4 of 4
Requests a TMS report from HUL's logistics team
Gets a weekly carrier file over email. By the time she reads it, the damage is done. No one has connected the carrier delay to the stockout risk in Blue Yonder.
