Usage Flow

How Your Team Uses the Platform

A simple daily workflow — from uploading data to making decisions — without switching between tools.

1
Log In and Upload Your Data
Each layer has a dedicated Upload page. Drag and drop your CSV — the platform auto-parses it and loads data across all pages instantly. No manual mapping or configuration required.
CSV Upload Auto-parse Dashboards update immediately
2
Monitor Live KPIs with Filters
Every layer has a dashboard showing its KPIs with trend indicators (green / amber / red). Apply filters — by warehouse (DEL, MUM, BLR), category, shift, date range, and more — to focus on what matters to your team right now.
Warehouse filter Date range Category Shift Green / Amber / Red status
3
Click a KPI to Drill Into Root Causes
Every KPI card and speedometer gauge is clickable. Click any metric to open a drill-down modal that shows the top data records driving that number — the specific shipment, SKU, or warehouse entry that is causing the result.
Click any KPI Top records displayed Ranked by impact
4
Review Automated Alerts
When a KPI breaches a threshold, the platform flags it on the dashboard card and on the dedicated Alerts page. The Planning Control Tower shows a health score for every supply chain node — Supplier, Inbound, Warehouse, Outbound, and Carrier — so nothing is missed.
Threshold-based alerts Alerts page Control Tower health view
5
Escalate via AI Voice Call or Chat
From any screen, tap the floating phone button to reach the right person — Logistics Manager, Transport Manager, Fleet Coordinator, or Carrier Relations Head. The AI places the call, streams a live transcript, and saves it to call history automatically. On the WDE and TMS layers, an AI chat assistant is also available to answer operational questions without leaving the screen.
Contact picker AI Voice Call Live transcript Call history AI Chat (WDE & TMS)
